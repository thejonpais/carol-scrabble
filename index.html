      📖 How to play

      1. Make words across or down. Your first word must cover the centre ★; after that, every word must join tiles already on the board.
      2. Tap a tile on your rack, then tap a square — or drag it. Tap a tile you just placed to take it back. Recall brings them all home.
      3. Coloured squares: 2L double letter, 3L triple letter, 2W double word, 3W triple word. They only count the turn you cover them.
      4. Use all 7 tiles in one go for a bingo: +50 points! 🌺
      5. The blank   can be any letter but scores 0.
      6. Swap trades tiles with the bag (needs 7+ left) and uses your turn. Six scoreless turns in a row end the game.
      7. In Challenge mode words aren’t checked automatically: the next player may tap ⚖️ Challenge. A wrong word comes off the board; a good word costs the challenger.
      8. The game ends when someone uses their last tile with the bag empty: they gain everyone else’s leftover tile points, and the others lose them.
      9. Tournament clock: each player has their own time bank; going over costs 10 points per started minute.

      🔍 On a phone, pinch or double-tap the board to zoom. Drag a tile — it floats above your finger so you can see where it lands.
